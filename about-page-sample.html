What is Whale Wallet?

Your whole money picture, boiled down to one number.

Why Whale Wallet is different

An app that shows its work.

We built Whale Wallet around trust: the numbers you see are the numbers in your data.

One number, not a wall of charts

Safe to spend per day until payday is the first thing you see.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Whale Wallet connect to my bank?

No. You add your cards and log spending by chat, quick entry or receipts. It never asks for your bank passwords.

Does it understand Tagalog?

Yes. Whale Chat understands English, Tagalog and Taglish, and you can type or use your voice.

Which AI does it use?

Google Gemini, and only after you agree. Your totals are calculated by the app, and any number in a reply is checked against your data.

Is there a mobile app?

A companion mobile app is being built. The website already works on your phone's browser.

Can I use other currencies?

Not yet. Whale Wallet is built for Philippine pesos.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings to export everything or delete your account together with its data and receipt images.
